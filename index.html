A free mosaic that plans on both axes.

Gallery Studio arranges images of different proportions without forcing them into a uniform grid. Its deterministic planner searches for a compact layout, preserves the source ratio and validates spacing before anything reaches the screen.

How the planning flow works
  1. The planner reads each original image ratio before folder sources are assigned to the gallery.
  2. Target density establishes nominal proportional tile sizes; candidate window and beam width control the two-dimensional search budget.
  3. High reverse factor, Harmonic spaces and Compositional style influence candidate scoring while the configured gap remains mandatory.
  4. After placement, Useful spaces derives calculation cells from the completed geometry and from the gallery edges. Cells may overlap.
  5. Cell conflicts are divided into exclusive boxes, giving every tile a non-overlapping area available for alignment.
  6. Tiles are centred inside their boxes in one final pass, then bounds, overlap and safety margins are validated.
  7. The validated geometry is rendered; locally selected image sources are attached on the following animation frame.

Design priorities

Compact, proportional, inspectable.

  1. 01

    Plan on both axes

    The search evaluates real two-dimensional cavities instead of flowing along one masonry axis.

  2. 02

    Preserve source ratios

    Every tile scales proportionally; the original image is never forced into a fixed crop or distorted shape.

  3. 03

    Separate placement from centring

    Overlapping calculation cells are resolved into exclusive boxes before tiles receive their final alignment.

  4. 04

    Inspect and validate

    Optional diagnostic overlays expose cells and boxes, while the geometry API checks bounds, overlap and the safety margin.

Minimal integration

Bring your own markup and images.

<div class="gallery-studio" data-gallery-studio>
  <figure class="gallery-studio__item" data-aspect="1.5">…</figure>
</div>

const gallery = GalleryStudio.mount('[data-gallery-studio]', {
  density: 3,
  gap: 12,
  candidateWindow: 8,
  beamWidth: 36,
  maxItemWidthRatio: 0.40,
  minItemScaleRatio: 0.55,
  compactionPressure: 0.06,
  highReverseFactor: true,
  harmonicSpaces: true,
  usefulSpaces: true,
  adaptiveSearch: true
});

The numerical engine is exposed separately as GovinFreeMosaicGeometry and can also be required from Node.js for tests or server-side planning.

Download kit

Copy your images, then choose their folder.

The kit already contains the loader. You do not need a manifest and you do not need to edit the source: put your images in one folder, open the demo and select that folder.

  1. Copy your images into a folder. 
    images/
  coast.jpg
  tower.webp
  field.png
  2. Open index.html.

    The included SVG illustrations appear first so the kit works immediately.

  3. Press Load image folder and choose the folder.

    The loader finds the image files, measures their real proportions and rebuilds the mosaic.

  4. Use the controls to retune the result.

    Folder order is preserved alphabetically; Rotate order lets you inspect another starting sequence.

The files stay on your device. The browser grants access only after you choose the folder, and the demo uses temporary local URLs: nothing is uploaded to a server. Chromium-based browsers provide the most complete folder-selection support.
